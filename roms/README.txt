Put the backup of your own disc (UMD) here, one folder per game:

  chains-of-olympus/   God of War: Chains of Olympus
  ghost-of-sparta/     God of War: Ghost of Sparta

An .iso or a .chd works. The file name doesn't matter: the disc is recognized by its ID (read with
gow_disc --id), and you are told when it is another game or sits in the wrong folder.

Discs that work:

  Chains of Olympus   UCUS98653 (USA, English only)
                      UCES00842 (Europe, with Spanish)
  Ghost of Sparta     UCUS98737 (USA, English, French and Spanish)
                      UCES01401 (Europe, with Spanish)

With both discs of a game, the game picker opens the European one, the one RetroAchievements knows. With one
disc, it opens that one.

Making the backup: on a PSP with custom firmware, its VSH menu ("USB Device: UMD Disc") shows the UMD as an
.iso over USB; copy it as it is. For a smaller file, a .chd made with "chdman createdvd" works too.

The project neither ships nor downloads any game. Git commits nothing from this folder but these notes.
